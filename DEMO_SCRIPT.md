# CovenantPulse 3-Minute Demo Script

## 0:00-0:20 - Login and Dashboard
1. Log in as `lender@example.com / Lender123!`.
2. Highlight the Portfolio Dashboard: due soon, overdue, waiting review, breaches, pending waivers.

## 0:20-1:30 - Loan Workspace + Workbench
3. Open **Loans** and select **Acme Manufacturing Term Loan**.
4. In **Overview**, show next due date and latest covenant results.
5. Switch to **Documents** tab and open a sample PDF; add a clause reference (page, snippet, tags).
6. Switch to **Workbench** tab and click **Run Extraction**.
7. Confirm one suggested obligation (shows creation + audit trail).

## 1:30-2:20 - Term Sheet + Drafts
8. Open **Term Sheet** tab and show v1 vs v2 (leverage threshold change).
9. Open **Drafts** tab and compare v1 vs v2 with **Semantic Diff**.

## 2:20-2:40 - Green Assessment + Consistency Check
10. Open **Green Assessment** tab, show score + verdict, and add an evidence tag.
11. Open **Consistency** tab and run the checker to show mismatch findings.

## 2:40-3:00 - Interoperability Export
12. Open **Exports** and generate CSV + PDF (show export history + open folder).
13. Export **Loan Obligation Schema (JSON)** with audit off.
14. (Optional) Open **Prototype Mode** to show the clickable flow hub.

Done.
