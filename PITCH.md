# CovenantPulse - Pitch

## Value Proposition
CovenantPulse is a desktop-first loan covenant and obligation compliance system that converts dense agreements into structured schedules, borrower submissions, automated covenant calculations, and audit-ready reporting - all offline and secure.

## Problem
Loan compliance is tracked in spreadsheets and email threads, leading to missed deadlines, slow approvals, and weak auditability. Lenders and borrowers lack a shared system of record.

## Solution
CovenantPulse provides:
- Structured obligations & covenant tracking
- Automated scheduling with reminders
- Borrower submissions with evidence uploads
- Lender review & waiver workflows
- Full audit trail and exportable compliance packs
- Document-to-Data Workbench for offline extraction + human review
- Term Sheet versioning, draft generation, and semantic diff
- Consistency checker to flag threshold and due-date drift

## Differentiation
- Desktop offline-first operation with local SQLite
- Audit-grade event log and role-based access control
- Rapid setup: import loan docs, tag clauses, generate schedules
- Integration-ready adapter layer for hackathon SDKs
- Loan Obligation Schema JSON export/import for interoperability

## Market
Lenders, credit funds, and corporate treasury teams managing complex loan portfolios - especially mid-market and syndicated loans.

## Business Model
SaaS + desktop licensing per lender portfolio, with optional managed compliance services for borrowers.

## Roadmap
- Clause extraction assistant with highlight tagging
- ESG obligation module for sustainability-linked loans
- Standardized Loan Obligation Schema export for interoperability
- Draft negotiation assistant with clause libraries and change tracking
